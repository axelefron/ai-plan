Business Service Card With QR Code

This card is used to set up your account linking your credit card with the QR Code. This will help you track it when lost.

When you notice that you don't have your card, DON'T CANCEL it!, PAUSE IT and follow these steps:

  1. Enter your portal through the app
  2. Click on "lost card" which will activate the "lost" status to that specific card
  3. Open the step-by-step guide on how to PAUSE your card on your specific bank provider
  4. Pause your card and wait until someone scans your card, without worrying strange charges
  5. Once someone scans it, we'll help you connect annonimously to arrange how to get the card back
  6. Chat through our chat interface integrated in the app and get your card back, with the option of giving a reward the person that contributed
  7. Indicate in the portal that you got it back, in order to change the status
  8. Open the guide on how to re-activate your card depending on your bank
  9. Done! Now you got it back safely without having to go trhough the process of canceling it and without any charges
